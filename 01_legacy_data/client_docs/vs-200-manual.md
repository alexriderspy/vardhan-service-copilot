# VS-200 Submersible Borewell Pump — Owner's Manual

## Overview
The Vardhan VS-200 is a multistage submersible pump for 4-inch (100 mm) borewells. The motor and pump sit under water inside the borewell and are controlled from a starter panel at ground level. Serial numbers start with `VS200-`.

## Specifications
- Motor: 1.5 HP (1.1 kW), single phase, 230 V, with control panel
- Stages: 12
- Maximum head: 90 metres
- Maximum flow: 3,000 litres per hour
- Borewell size: minimum 100 mm inner diameter
- Maximum sand content: 50 grams per cubic metre

## Installation
Lower the pump with a stainless steel safety rope; never hang it by the cable. The pump must sit at least 3 metres below the lowest dynamic water level and at least 1 metre above the bottom of the borewell. Install the control panel in a dry, shaded place. Installation must be done by a Vardhan-trained technician for the warranty to be valid.

## Troubleshooting: unusual noise or vibration
Submersible pumps are normally almost silent at the surface. A knocking sound in the delivery pipe usually means water hammer: fit a non-return valve near the borewell head. Grinding noise or sudden vibration means sand or silt has entered the pump stages; stop the pump and book a service visit, because continued running will wear out the impellers.

## Troubleshooting: low pressure or low flow
Low flow is usually caused by a falling water level in the borewell, a clogged inlet screen, or worn stages from sandy water. Check whether the problem is worse in summer (water level). The control panel's ammeter reading helps the technician diagnose the cause.

## Troubleshooting: motor overheating or tripping
The control panel trips on dry run, overload or low voltage. Frequent dry-run trips mean the water level has dropped below the pump; the pump may need to be lowered. Overload trips with a high ammeter reading mean the pump is jammed with sand.

## Troubleshooting: water leakage
Leakage at the surface is almost always in the delivery pipe joints or the non-return valve, not in the pump itself.

## Maintenance schedule
- Every 3 months: check the control panel ammeter reading and note it down.
- Every year: inspect the cable joint and the safety rope (service visit).
- Every 3 to 4 years: pull out the pump and check the stages if the borewell has sand.
