# Vardhan Pumps & Services — Warranty Policy

## Warranty period
Every new Vardhan pump is covered from the date of installation (or the invoice date, if the installation date is not recorded):
- VP-100 centrifugal pump: 24 months
- VS-200 submersible pump: 18 months
- VB-300 pressure booster pump: 24 months

Repaired or replaced parts are covered for the rest of the original warranty period or 6 months, whichever is longer.

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship: motor winding failure under normal voltage, defective impellers, defective mechanical seals within the first 12 months, and pressure switch failure on the VB-300. Covered repairs include parts and labour, and the service visit is free.

## What is not covered
- Damage from dry running (running the pump without water)
- Damage from voltage fluctuation when no stabiliser or control panel was used
- Sand or silt damage in VS-200 pumps when the borewell sand content is above 50 grams per cubic metre
- Repairs or opening of the pump by anyone other than a Vardhan technician
- Normal wear parts after 12 months: mechanical seals, capacitors, strainers
- Physical damage, fire, flood or lightning

## How to make a warranty claim
Email support with the pump serial number, the invoice or order number, a short description of the problem, and photos or a video if possible. Our team checks the installation date in our records, confirms whether the pump is in warranty, and books a technician visit. Claims are usually decided within 2 working days after the technician's inspection.

## Service visit charges
Visits for pumps in warranty are free when the fault is covered. Out-of-warranty visits inside Pune city limits cost Rs 750 per visit plus parts; outside city limits Rs 1,200 per visit plus parts. The visit charge is waived if the customer goes ahead with a repair costing more than Rs 3,000.

## Extended warranty
Customers can buy a 12-month extended warranty (Vardhan Care) for Rs 1,999 before the original warranty ends. It covers the same faults as the standard warranty and includes one free preventive maintenance visit.
