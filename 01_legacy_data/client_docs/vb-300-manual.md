# VB-300 Pressure Booster Pump — Owner's Manual

## Overview
The Vardhan VB-300 is an automatic pressure booster pump for apartments, bungalows and small offices. It boosts low municipal or overhead-tank pressure for showers, washing machines and upper floors. It starts and stops automatically using a pressure switch and a pressure tank. Serial numbers start with `VB300-`.

## Specifications
- Motor: 0.5 HP (0.37 kW), single phase, 230 V
- Cut-in pressure: 1.5 bar; cut-out pressure: 2.8 bar
- Maximum flow: 2,400 litres per hour
- Pressure tank: 8 litres, pre-charge 1.3 bar
- Protection: built-in dry-run sensor

## Installation
Install the VB-300 after the overhead tank outlet or the main supply line, with a bypass line so water still flows when the pump is under service. Keep the pump indoors or under a cover, away from rain.

## Troubleshooting: unusual noise or vibration
A clicking sound with the pump starting and stopping every few seconds is called short cycling. It means the pressure tank has lost its air charge. Check the tank pre-charge with a tyre gauge (it should be 1.3 bar with the pump off and taps open). A whining noise means air in the line; open the highest tap in the house for 2 minutes.

## Troubleshooting: low pressure or low flow
Check that the inlet strainer is clean and the overhead tank has water. If the pump runs but pressure stays below 1.5 bar, the pressure switch may need recalibration by a technician.

## Troubleshooting: pump does not start
Check the power supply and the dry-run sensor light. If the overhead tank was empty, the dry-run protection locks the pump for 15 minutes; it restarts on its own after that.

## Troubleshooting: motor overheating or tripping
Continuous running without stopping means a leak somewhere in the house plumbing (a running toilet cistern is the most common cause) or a faulty pressure switch.

## Maintenance schedule
- Every 3 months: clean the inlet strainer.
- Every 6 months: check the pressure tank pre-charge.
- Every year: general service visit (pressure switch and seal check).
